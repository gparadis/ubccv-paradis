## 8. TEACHING

### (a) Areas of special interest and accomplishments

I specialize in operational research, mathematical modelling, forest management planning, and forest engineering, integrating academic and real-world insights. My focus includes forest ecosystem process modelling, forest resource analysis, forest estate modelling, forest economics, and environmental sustainability, notably carbon accounting for forest ecosystems and forest products. A key accomplishment is developing FRST 422, a capstone course linking theory with practice, enhancing students' critical thinking and problem-solving skills. This course also serves as a precursor to FRST 423. Additionally, I am creating a new module in FRST 558 on forest management and resource analysis, leveraging my experience as a forester, engineer, and forest estate modelling expert. Both FRST 422 and FRST 558 aim to equip students with essential knowledge and technical skills for their future careers as forest professionals.

## 9. SCHOLARLY AND PROFESSIONAL ACTIVITIES

### (a) Areas of special interest and accomplishments

My research focuses on forest management problems, applying operations research (OR) methods to formulate and solve complex decision problems through mathematical optimization. I use a systems approach to model interactions between forest ecosystems, industrial supply chains, governments, and society. My interests intersect forest science, economics, engineering, data science, computer science, and OR. Key research topics include forest estate modeling, cumulative effects modeling, supply chain modeling, carbon accounting, and nature-based decarbonization. As Theme Leader, I launched the Natural Capital and Biodiversity research theme within the Bradshaw Research Initiative for Minerals and Mining (BRIMM), mobilizing government and industry partners for impactful research. I’ve secured substantial amounts of “soft” funding from key government agencies and established firm commitments for future funding. Additionally, I am negotiating new industry partnerships and have applied for major infrastructure grant (CFI JELF) to support advanced research computing equipment for my modeling research program (and development of an open innovation hub).

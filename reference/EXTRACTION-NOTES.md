# Notes on the import from `reference/ubccv_gparadis-nohighlighting.docx`

The Word document was parsed from `word/document.xml` directly rather than via
a Markdown conversion, because the CV uses literal `*` (presenting author) and
`^` (HQP) characters in author lists that collide with Markdown emphasis. Bold
and italic were read from run properties, where there is no ambiguity.

## Corrections applied

Three typos in the Word original were repaired on import. They are listed in
`TYPO_FIXES` in `tools/docx_to_bib.py`.

1. **`M acFarlane, D.` → `MacFarlane, D.`**
   A stray tab from Word's line wrapping had been saved into the middle of the
   surname, in the Munro et al. (2025) *Journal of Forestry* author list. This
   is why `acfarlane` still shows up in `make check`: the original token no
   longer exists.

2. **`Lebel L. (2010)` → `Lebel, L. (2010)`**
   Missing comma, in "Paradis, G.*, Laliberté, F. and Lebel L. (2010). Impact
   de la coupe partielle…". Without the comma the name could not be parsed and
   the entry lost both its co-author and its year.

3. **`Sharma,` → `{Sharma}`** — *needs your attention*
   In the same Munro et al. author list, the original reads
   `… Restrepo, H., Sharma, Shaw, J., Smith-Mateja, E. …`. Sharma has no
   initial. It is recorded as `{Sharma}`, which BibTeX renders as a
   single-token name, so the author is not lost. **Please supply the missing
   initial** in `data/publications.bib` when you know it.

## Things worth reviewing

These came through mechanically and are correct as extracted, but are worth a
read since they were irregular in the original:

- **Duplicate list numbers.** The original numbered two entries `26` under
  Other Presentations and two entries `36` under Non-Refereed Publications.
  Both pairs are distinct entries and were kept; the renderer now numbers them
  consecutively, so the counts shifted (57 and 46 rather than 55 and 44).
- **Courses taught, 2022–2023.** The academic-year banner is present with no
  course rows beneath it. Rendered as *(no courses taught)*.
- **Section 6(b), "At UBC"** includes "Professeur associé (École de foresterie,
  Université de Moncton), 2023/01–2025/12", which is not a UBC appointment.
- **Section 12** in the original is numbered twice in the SAC Guide itself
  (both "Service to the Community" and "Awards and Distinctions"). This CV
  follows your existing numbering: 10 University, 11 Community, 12 Awards,
  13 Other.
- **Venue strings** for a few presentations were free-form in the original and
  were kept whole in the `venue` field rather than split into structured parts.
